DABBAWALATIME PREDICTION

Mumbai's dabbawalas deliver well over a hundred thousand home-cooked tiffins across the city every single day, with almost no errors. This tool uses a machine learning model trained on real dabbawala logistics data — routes, tiffin details, distances, and workloads — to estimate how long a delivery will take before the tiffin even leaves the kitchen.

Our goal: give dispatchers and customers a fast, data-backed estimate of delivery time, so every tiffin reaches its dabba on schedule.
